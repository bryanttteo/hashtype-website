# HashType — brand

## Name
- Written as **HashType** (one word, capital H and T). Never "Hashtype", "Hash Type" or "#Type".

## One-line description (footer, social profiles)
HashType helps businesses run leaner with AI-first automation, IoT and vision AI.

## Positioning
- **AI-first:** we design new workflows around what AI makes possible, rather than turning old processes into digital copies.
- **Practical:** deployable solutions that run on the ground, not slide decks.
- **Outcomes before technology:** lead with cost, time, risk and compliance; explain the technology second.

## Voice
- Confident, practical, jargon-light. Written for SME owners and operations, plant and facilities managers.
- Short sentences. Active voice. "We" for HashType, "you" for the reader.
- Every claim must survive "prove it". No superlatives ("leading", "best-in-class", "revolutionary").
- Avoid: "synergy", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "unlock", "seamless", "next-gen".
- British spelling (organise, optimise, colour).

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #000000 | Every page and section. Never light. |
| Surface | #0D1013 | Cards and panels |
| Hairline | #26313A | Borders and dividers |
| Mint (primary) | #7EF0C1 | Primary button fill (black text), links, highlights. One mint-filled button per page. |
| Teal | #4FB3A9 | Secondary accents, small labels |
| Indigo | #5466AE | Gradients, borders, glows, large display only. Never body text. |
| Text | #E8ECEE | Body text |
| Muted | #9AA6AD | Secondary text |

Signature element: the logo's mint-to-indigo gradient, used sparingly (hero glow, thin rule under section titles).

## Type
- Headings, UI labels and buttons: **IBM Plex Mono** (500 and 600 weights)
- Body text: **IBM Plex Sans** (400, and 500 for emphasis)
- Both are free under the SIL Open Font License. Self-host the `.woff2` files from `public/fonts/`; no external font requests.
- Fallbacks: `'IBM Plex Mono', Menlo, Consolas, monospace` and `'IBM Plex Sans', -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`.
- Body text at least 16 px. Headings use slight negative letter-spacing (-0.01em); small mono labels use positive spacing (0.06em) in capitals.

## Logo
- File: `images/hashtype-logo.png` (transparent PNG, 1048 × 1008 px).
- Always on black. Never on a white or light card, never recoloured, stretched or given a drop shadow.
- Header and footer lockup: the mark with "HashType Technologies" set in the heading font beside it.
- Minimum size: 32 px tall on screen.

## Imagery
- Real places and real work: ports, warehouses, plants, sites, kitchens.
- Prefer darker or dusk shots that sit naturally on black.
- No stock "AI brain", glowing-robot or robot-hand art. No visible third-party logos.
