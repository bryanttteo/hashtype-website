## [solutions]
- **Heading:** Solutions

### Bespoke Business Applications {#bespoke}
- **Outcome:** Software that fits how your business actually runs, instead of forcing your team to work around an off-the-shelf tool.
- **How it works:** We map your workflow, then build a custom system around it, with AI built in where it saves effort.
- **Interface sketch (labelled "Illustration"):** Jobs · This week · rows: Pump service, Panel upgrade, Site survey, Filter replacement · chips: New / In progress / Done · message: "Suggested: assign to Team B"

### Digital Enablement {#digital-enablement}
- **Outcome:** No more chasing paper forms and spreadsheet versions. Everyone works from the same live information.
- **How it works:** We move manual, paper and spreadsheet processes onto connected digital workflows.
- **Interface sketch (labelled "Illustration"):** Daily inspection · Tablet · "Paper" sheet fades out · checklist: Fire exits clear, Extinguishers checked, First-aid kit stocked · signature line "Signed" · message: "Synced to the office"

### Intelligence via IoT {#iot}
- **Outcome:** Know what's happening on your equipment and sites in real time, and get alerted before small problems become costly ones.
- **How it works:** Sensors capture the data; we turn it into dashboards, alerts and insights.
- **Interface sketch (labelled "Illustration"):** Pump room · Live · tiles: Temperature, Vibration, Power · chips: Normal / Watch · message: "Pump 2 vibration rising. Maintenance notified."

### Vision AI {#vision-ai}
- **Outcome:** Cameras that watch for what matters, so people don't have to stare at screens.
- **How it works:** Camera-based detection, inspection and monitoring, with alerts when something needs attention.
- **Interface sketch (labelled "Illustration"):** Line camera · Packing line · items tagged OK / Scratch, flagged item set aside · message: "Defect caught before packing"

### Agentic Automation {#agentic-automation}
- **Outcome:** Multi-step tasks handled end to end, with your people approving the decisions that need a human.
- **How it works:** AI agents work across your tools to complete tasks, and pause for approval where you set it.
- **Interface sketch (labelled "Illustration"):** AI agent · Purchase request · steps: Read supplier email, Draft purchase order, Check budget · Manager approval (Approve / Reject; Waiting → Approved) · Order sent · message: "Order sent to supplier"
